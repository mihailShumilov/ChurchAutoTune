# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mihailShumilov/ChurchAutoTune/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the *Bug report* form.
- **Hardware check results:** open an issue with the *Hardware check result* form.
- **Security or safety problems:** report them privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

ChurchTune is pre-alpha and maintained by volunteers in their spare time. There is no paid support.
English, Russian and Ukrainian are welcome.
